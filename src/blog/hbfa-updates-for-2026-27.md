The 2026–27 [Harmonized Base Funding Agreement](https://physiology.utoronto.ca/2026-27-temerty-faculty-medicine-harmonized-base-funding-agreement) sets base funding, award top-ups, and funding terms for eligible doctoral-stream MSc and PhD students in participating Temerty Faculty of Medicine graduate units.

## 2026–27 base funding

The published 2026–27 totals, including living allowance, tuition and fees, and UHIP where applicable, are:

- Domestic MSc: **$40,308.67**
- Domestic PhD: $42,191.08
- International MSc: $67,116.67
- International PhD: $43,139.08

_Students should confirm their individual package and eligibility with their graduate unit._

## How award top-ups work

Awards requiring a student application may qualify for a supervisor top-up. The cumulative award amount determines how the award affects base funding:

1. $0–$2,000: no top-up; the student keeps awards up to a combined $2,000 without reducing base funding.
2. $2,001–$9,999: awards contribute to base funding and the student receives a $2,000 top-up.
3. $10,000–$15,000: awards contribute to base funding and the student receives a $3,000 top-up.
4. $15,001–$39,999: awards contribute to base funding and the student receives a $4,000 top-up.
5. $40,000–$45,000: awards contribute to base funding and the student receives a $5,000 top-up, subject to a $50,000 combined maximum.
6. $45,001–$50,000: no top-up; the student may retain awards up to $50,000.

:::callout{kind="information" title="Annual top-up limit"}
Within one academic year or 12-month period, cumulative top-ups cannot exceed $5,000.
:::

:::callout{kind="important" title="Some funding is excluded"}
Top-up eligibility excludes University of Toronto Fellowship awards such as UTF Open, Doctoral Completion Awards, Temerty Faculty of Medicine Entrance Scholarships, GSS funds, travel awards, and bursaries.
:::

:::callout{kind="warning" title="Check your eligibility"}
This agreement excludes students receiving funding above base funding while registered in specified physician-scientist or MD research fellowship training programs. Confirm eligibility with your graduate unit.
:::
