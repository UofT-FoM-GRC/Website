# Website content management

Shared language for creating, reviewing, publishing, and maintaining GRC website content.

## Workflow

**Content draft**:
An unpublished content change saved for review.

**Site preview**:
A reviewable rendering of a content draft using the same presentation rules as the public website. This is the authoritative preview.
_Avoid_: Preview, CMS preview

**Editor preview**:
Immediate feedback shown inside the editing interface. It may help with writing, but it is not authoritative.

**Publish**:
Make a reviewed content draft public. Publishing is distinct from saving a draft and from generating a site preview.

**Self-publishing**:
An author may publish their own reviewed content draft without approval from another person.

**Archive**:
Remove published content from listings and discovery while preserving access through its stable public address. Archiving is not deletion.

**Expiry**:
Automatic removal of time-limited content from listings and discovery after its selected Toronto calendar date. Expired content remains available through its stable public address.

## Content

**Long-form content**:
Editorial material whose structure varies by subject, specifically blog bodies and detailed resource-page content. Fixed site settings and concise records are not long-form content.

**Content block**:
A reusable, purpose-specific unit editors can insert and reorder without controlling its visual styling. Examples include text, a callout, an image, a link group, or a sequence of steps.

**Callout**:
A content block that separates information needing special attention from surrounding text. Its semantic kind is **information**, **important**, or **warning**; appearance follows that meaning rather than an editor-selected color.

**Resource section**:
A named, directly linkable division of a resource page. Its identity remains stable after publication.

**Resource card**:
A grouped item within a resource section. Its details may be composed from content blocks while the section controls placement.

**Image description**:
Text conveying an image's relevant content or purpose to people who cannot see it. Every image added through the editing interface requires an image description.
_Avoid_: Image caption

## Roles

**Content editor**:
The annually designated GRC member who manages routine site content through the editing interface. A content editor is not expected to understand repositories, branches, pull requests, or code.
_Avoid_: Webmaster

**Power user**:
A contributor who deliberately works with content source syntax and accepts responsibility for validating custom presentation. Power-user controls remain outside the routine editing interface.

**Technical steward**:
The continuing former GRC director who serves as first contact for rare access, recovery, conflict, hosting, or code emergencies. The technical steward is not part of routine content publishing.
